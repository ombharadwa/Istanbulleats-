Our Story

Mumbai roots, Juhapura table.

Istanbul Eats started in Mumbai and brought the same idea to Ahmedabad: one kitchen, every craving. Wood-fired pizzas sit on the same menu as Turkish shawarma, grill burgers, tacos, and loaded sandwiches — made fresh to order, not pulled from a warmer.

Whether you're in for a quick zinger burger or a full Special Istanbul Pizza with the table, this is the neighbourhood's one-stop order.
